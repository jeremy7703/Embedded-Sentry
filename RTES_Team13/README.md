### How to Use:
- Main Menu: You can click either record to record the gesture key or unlock.
- Training Phase: You need to repeat your gesture twice so that the gesture charactistics can be learned.
- Unlocking Phase: Simply repeat your gesture to unlock.
- Reset Key: Press Blue Button to reset your gesture key.
